---
name: UniPod operations policy
description: User-stated workflow and operating rules for the Innovation Hub's UniPod Operations area.
---

- Students and members of the public submit briefs to reception; reception forwards each brief to the relevant UniPod department.
- Partner work requires a signed MoU from UniPod before it can be treated as approved.
- Trainees pay for each training session using the published bank details and submit proof of payment.
- Training is delivered in scheduled classes by assigned instructors; there are no full-time members.
- UniPod equipment stays on-site, and repair issues are surfaced to both managers.

**Why:** These rules were supplied by the user as requirements for the Operations area.

**How to apply:** Keep them reflected in reception routing, partner approval, per-session payment review, class scheduling, and the equipment register.
